SOCIAL CHANGE PROJECT

Z | Published 2026

MENTAL
HEALTH PH

When silent struggles, academic burnout, and social stigma weigh heavily on Filipino youth, #MentalHealthPH ("Tara, #UsapTayo!") builds a safe, compassionate digital community where every student is heard, supported, and empowered to heal.

▶ Play

THE PROBLEM

Filipino students and young people frequently experience overwhelming academic pressure, emotional exhaustion, social isolation, and anxiety. Despite these daily struggles, deep-rooted cultural stigma, fear of being judged or invalidated, and limited access to youth-friendly safe spaces prevent many teenagers from speaking up or seeking professional help.

WHY MENTAL HEALTH PH MATTERS & HOW WE WILL SOLVE IT

MentalHealthPH matters because it turns silence into solidarity through its "Tara, #UsapTayo!" advocacy—reminding every student that their feelings are valid and that no one has to carry their struggles alone. By joining and amplifying this movement, we will solve the problem through student-led awareness campaigns, judgment-free peer support circles, accessible crisis directories, and daily wellness check-in habits.

01
TARA, #USAPTAYO CAMPAIGNS

Launch student-led social media threads and campus storytelling boards that normalize open conversations about stress, burnout, and emotional well-being.

02
SAFE SPACE PEER CIRCLES

Organize judgment-free peer listening sessions and online support huddles where students can express their feelings openly and feel genuinely heard.

03
ACCESSIBLE HELP & HOTLINE GUIDE

Share easy-to-access digital guides featuring verified Philippine mental health crisis hotlines, school guidance contacts, and free tele-counseling services.

04
DAILY WELLNESS CHECK-IN

Encourage classmates and friends to practice mindful daily check-ins, active listening, and connecting peers in distress with trusted adults and professionals.
